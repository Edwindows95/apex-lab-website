Sleep & Recovery

Recovery is the real performance.

Overnight HRV, deep sleep, and morning resting heart rate tell you whether your body truly recharged — or carried today's load into tomorrow.

What does overnight HRV actually tell me about recovery?

Overnight HRV is the honest measure of whether your autonomic nervous system recovered. Higher HRV means your body de-escalated the day's stress; lower HRV means today's load carried into tomorrow.

Why don't I feel rested after eight hours of sleep?

Sleep quantity is only part of the story. Fragmented deep sleep, reduced REM, or an elevated overnight heart rate mean your body and brain stayed partially "on" all night — and Apex Lab makes that visible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How it works

Physiological data from your wearable — heart rate, stress, Body Battery, respiration, sleep, and optionally HRV. We also integrate your work calendar (ICS format) to map activities to health responses.

Apex Lab supports Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Fitbit, and Labfront. Most modern models from these brands are compatible — we can advise on the specifics for your device before starting.

A typical measurement period runs for 2 to 4 weeks. This gives us enough data to identify meaningful patterns in stress, recovery, and work-related health responses. Longer tracking is available for longitudinal insights.

Privacy

No. Individual reports belong to the participant. If an organization commissions the study, they only receive anonymized, aggregate-level insights — never personal data. Privacy is fundamental to how Apex Lab operates.

Yes. All data handling follows GDPR requirements. We only collect data that is necessary for the analysis, participants give explicit informed consent, and data is stored securely with full transparency on processing.
